## Background

DUSt3R is a state-of-the-art feed-forward 3D reconstruction model. This project focuses on creating lightweight variants through knowledge distillation and architectural optimization, enabling deployment on resource-constrained platforms.

## My Contributions

- Lightweight model architecture design (Tiny/Small/Mid variants)
- Knowledge distillation pipeline from teacher to student models
- Model compression and optimization for edge deployment

## Technical Details

**Base Model**: DUSt3R (feed-forward 3D reconstruction)
**Approach**: Knowledge distillation, model compression
**Variants**: Tiny (~22M), Small (~64M), Mid (~105M)
**Framework**: PyTorch, CUDA
